✦ 3D Geometric Transformation Visualizer ✦

Mathematics has always lived in the abstract. Transformations, matrices, eigenvectors—they exist as pure numbers on a page. But what if they had a face? A voice? A dance?

This is an interactive AR/VR environment where abstract mathematical concepts transcend the textbook and become intuitive visual experiences. Drag a cube, watch it rotate in real-time. Pinch your fingers, see the scale change. Tilt your phone, witness geometric transformations unfold in your physical space.

The Journey

What began as a simple three-dimensional cube evolved into a dynamic platform supporting:

  • Five Platonic solids (cube, tetrahedron, octahedron, dodecahedron, icosahedron)
  • Real-time transformation matrices (translation, rotation, scaling)
  • Linear algebra decomposition and composition
  • Hand gesture recognition (pinch, grab, swipe)
  • Device gimbal control (accelerometer-based)
  • Augmented reality with Hiro marker detection
  • Coordinate space visualization (model, world, view, clip)
