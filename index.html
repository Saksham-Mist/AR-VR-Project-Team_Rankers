3D Geometric Transformation Visualization Environment

An interactive 3D environment that transforms abstract mathematical concepts into intuitive visual experiences.

What started as a simple cube-based transformation system evolved into a dynamic platform supporting multiple geometries and real-time interaction, enhanced further with Augmented Reality.
